# Re-order Warnings

Cart Compass checks what you're looking at against everything you've ordered before.

## On a product page

A panel appears in the corner when you've bought the product before:

> **You've ordered this before:** 6 units in 2 orders, 2 still on the way

It lists each past order with its date, quantity, variant and status. Close it with **×**.

It also catches the **same part from a different store**. Listings often share a model or part number (*N16R8*, *W25Q128*, *NE555*…), so when a product from another store shares one with something you bought, the panel says *"You've likely ordered this from another store"* and lists the match under **Same item from other stores**. Items that are only similar by title are listed separately.

If you ordered the product but the order expired without shipping, the panel says so: *"Never shipped: your order of ×1 on 2026-10-02 expired"*. That way you know you still don't have it.

## In your cart and at checkout

Items you've bought before get a badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ordered 3× before** | You've ordered this exact product. |
| **Ordered 3× before · 1 in transit** | Some of it is still on the way. |
| **Likely ordered (other store)** | Same model or part number from another store. |

Hover over a badge to see the past orders.

## What counts

- Orders must be saved in Cart Compass first. Run a sync if a warning seems missing.
- Cancelled orders don't count as "ordered", so they don't trigger warnings. Expired orders are mentioned separately, as above.
- Orders removed from your online AliExpress list by clean-up still count.
