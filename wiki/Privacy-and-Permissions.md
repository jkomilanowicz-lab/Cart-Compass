# Privacy and Permissions

Cart Compass has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Your order history is stored in your browser and goes only where you send it: an export file, or a Google Sheet in your own Google account.

The full policy is in [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/jkomilanowicz-lab/Cart-Compass/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Read and change data on aliexpress.com / aliexpress.us** | To read your order list and order pages, show re-order warnings, and remove orders from your list when you run clean-up. |
| **Storage** | To save your orders in your browser. *Unlimited storage* is there because long histories can exceed the default quota. |
| **Tabs** | To find or open your orders tab when you click Sync, and to read order detail pages in a background tab. |
| **Scripting** | To attach to an AliExpress tab that was already open when the extension was installed or updated. |
| **script.google.com** *(optional)* | Requested only when you set up the Google Sheet, to send your orders to the script in your own account. |

## Your Google Sheet key

The sheet key is stored only in your browser. It's shown masked in Settings, and it's never included in exports or page samples.
