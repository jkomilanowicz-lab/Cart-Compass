# Getting Started

The first run loads your whole order history. After that, a quick sync now and then keeps it current.

## 1. Load your orders

1. Sign in to AliExpress and open **My Orders**: <https://www.aliexpress.com/p/order/index.html>. Orders already on screen are saved straight away.
2. Click the Cart Compass icon, then **Full sync**.
3. Leave the AliExpress tab open until the message in its corner says *Done*. Cart Compass clicks **View orders** until your whole history has loaded. With hundreds of orders this takes a few minutes.

## 2. Fetch order details

The order list only shows short titles and totals. Each order's own page has the full item titles, variants, unit prices, shipping, tax, discounts and tracking numbers.

1. In the popup, click **Fetch details**.
2. Cart Compass opens order pages one at a time in a background tab, a few seconds apart, reads them and closes them. You can keep browsing.
3. Each run does up to 150 orders (changeable in [[Settings]]). Click **Fetch details** again until the popup shows **0 still need details**.

Click **Stop** at any time; nothing is lost.

## 3. Optional: set up the Google Sheet

A Google Sheet gives you a readable copy of everything and acts as a backup. It's also required before clean-up removes completed orders. See **[[Google Sheet Setup]]** (about 5 minutes).

## Day to day

- **Quick sync** picks up new orders and stops as soon as it reaches ones it already has.
- **Fetch details** again now and then: it re-reads orders that are still on their way, so statuses and tracking stay current, and checks closed orders for refunds.
- Browse AliExpress as usual. [[Re-order Warnings]] appear on product pages and in your cart.
