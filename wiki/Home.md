# Cart Compass wiki

**Cart Compass** keeps a private record of every AliExpress order you place, warns you before you buy something twice, and can mirror everything to a Google Sheet you own.

Everything runs in your own browser. There's no Cart Compass server, no account and no tracking.

## Start here

1. **[[Installing]]:** add the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **[[Getting Started]]:** load your order history and its details (about 10 minutes the first time).
3. **[[Google Sheet Setup]]** *(recommended)*: a live, readable copy of your orders, which doubles as a backup.
